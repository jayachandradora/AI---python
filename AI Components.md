Sometimes you have to draw it out to see the big picture. 

The AI landscape can get overwhelming with new terminology dropping every day. I find it helpful to visualize it as layers of capability.

We started with Classical AI (Rules), moved to Deep Learning (Patterns), exploded with Generative AI (Creation), and are now entering the era of Agentic AI (Action).

The top layer is where the magic happens next—giving AI the ability to plan and use tools.

A simple sketch to explain a complex evolution.
